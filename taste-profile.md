# Henry's Wine Taste Profile

*Last updated: 2026-03-01 (from Vivino data — 113 wines, 32 rated)*

## What Henry Likes (from his own reviews)

**Oak and structure** — approves of oak in nearly every top review. Vanilla, leather, cedar. Likes wines with backbone that open up after 1-3 hours breathing.

**Medium+ body, not bombs** — structure and complexity over raw power. Pahlmeyer at 15.5% was "too full on". Prefers 13-14.5%.

**Not too fruit-forward** — wants complexity beyond fruit. "Very fruit forward and bold" was a negative.

**Values quality-to-price ratio** — "drinks like it's 3x the price" and "great value for the money" appear often.

## Do NOT Buy

- **Gusbourne** — cellar full, no more
- Wines he already owns or has rated — check history/ first

## Dislikes

- Jammy/fruit-forward without complexity
- High alcohol (15%+)
- Boring/generic Bordeaux at inflated prices
- "Pleasant" wines that don't make you think

## Known Comfort Zones

Henry knows and enjoys these well. Use them as **anchors** (1-2 per case max) not the whole case:
- California/Oregon Pinot Noir
- South African reds (Stellenbosch, Swartland, Franschhoek)
- Rioja Reserva/Gran Reserva
- Oaked California Chardonnay (buttery, RRV-style)

## Where to Push Him

Henry has barely explored these but his palate suggests he'd love them. **At least 2-3 picks per case should come from outside his comfort zone:**
- **Northern Rhône** — Crozes-Hermitage, Saint-Joseph, Cornas. He likes peppery structured Syrah from SA — the originals should thrill him.
- **Piedmont beyond Barolo** — Barbaresco, Nebbiolo d'Alba, Roero. He rated a Barolo 4.0 but said it was light — try a richer producer or Barbaresco.
- **Portugal** — Douro reds, Dão, Alentejo. Complex, structured, great value. Right up his street.
- **Greece** — Xinomavro (Naoussa), Assyrtiko. He scanned a Greek white, barely scratched the surface.
- **Southern France** — Languedoc, Roussillon, Bandol. Serious wines at fraction of Bordeaux prices.
- **Austrian whites** — Grüner Veltliner, Austrian Riesling. He's only tracked NZ Sauv Blanc for whites outside Chardonnay.
- **Sicily/Etna** — Nerello Mascalese. Pinot-like reds from volcanic soil.
- **Argentina beyond Malbec** — Cabernet Franc from Uco Valley.
- **Sherry** — He likes complex, oxidative styles (d'Yquem, old Rioja). Amontillado or Palo Cortado could be a revelation.
- **Jura** — Vin Jaune, Savagnin. Same logic as sherry.
- **Lebanon/Georgia** — He rated Musar 4.5 and tracked Saperavi. More from both.
- **English still wines** — beyond Gusbourne sparkling, the still Pinot and Chardonnay scene is maturing

## Important: Variety Is the Point

**Do not pick 6 wines from the same region or retailer.** The whole point is surprise and discovery. Each case should span at least 4 countries. Mix retailers — Majestic, BBR, and Wine Society all have different strengths. Include at least one white and ideally something unexpected.
