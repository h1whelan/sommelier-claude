## Known Preferences

### General Style
- Tends toward full-bodied reds and aromatic whites
- Appreciates complexity and structure over simple fruit-forward wines
- Open to trying new regions and grapes — not a creature of habit

### Red Wines
- Enjoys bold, structured reds — Cabernet Sauvignon, Malbec, Syrah/Shiraz
- Appreciates good Burgundy (Pinot Noir) when quality justifies price
- Italian reds: Barolo, Brunello, Chianti Classico
- Spanish reds: Rioja (reserva/gran reserva), Ribera del Duero
- Interested in discovering: Greek, Portuguese, Georgian reds

### White Wines
- Aromatic varieties: Riesling, Gewurztraminer, Viognier
- Quality Chablis and white Burgundy
- Not a fan of heavily oaked Chardonnay
- Enjoys crisp Albariño and Grüner Veltliner in summer

### Other
- Open to natural and low-intervention wines when well-made
- Enjoys rosé in summer (Provence style preferred)
- Interested in sparkling beyond Champagne (Crémant, English sparkling, Cava)

## Price Range
- Sweet spot: £12-25 per bottle
- Will spend more (£30-50) for special occasions
- Appreciates good value — price-to-quality ratio matters

## Retailers
- **Primary:** Majestic (convenience + mixed case discounts)
- **Also shops at:** Berry Bros & Rudd, independent wine shops, Waitrose
