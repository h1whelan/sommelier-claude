## Palate Profile — What Henry Actually Likes

### Strong patterns from reviews:

1. **Oak and vanilla are positive markers** — mentions oak approvingly in nearly every top-rated wine. Likes American and French oak influence. Does NOT dislike oak (unlike the initial profile suggested).

2. **Buttery Chardonnay is a favourite** — Journeyman, Hartford Court, Donnachadh, Calyptra, Robertson Winery all rated highly. Specifically likes Russian River Valley and Californian Chardonnay. Prefers these over white Burgundy (rated Sylvain Pataille Bourgogne Blanc 4.0 but said "a west coast US Chardonnay beats it").

3. **Wines that open up with time** — frequently notes improvement after 1-3 hours breathing. Appreciates wines with complexity that reveals itself. Not impatient.

4. **Medium+ to full body preferred, but not extreme** — rated Pahlmeyer Jayson Red 4.1 but said "still a little too full on for me" at 15.5%. Likes structure but not alcohol bombs.

5. **Pinot Noir enthusiast** — Merry Edwards (4.7), Sea Smoke (4.7), Storm (multiple), Au Bon Climat (4.2). Gravitates toward Oregon and California Pinot, plus South African.

6. **South Africa is a major region** — huge number of SA wines scanned. Boekenhoutskloof, Porseleinberg, Saronsberg, Storm, Kanonkop, Crystallum, Graceland. Clearly spent time there (mentions "La Cotte Inn in Franschhoek"). SA Pinotage, Syrah, Bordeaux blends, Cab Sav all explored.

7. **Spanish Rioja and Ribera del Duero** — consistent favourites. López de Heredia, Viña Ardanza, Viña Pedrosa, López de Haro all rated well. Likes reserva/gran reserva styles.

8. **Dessert wine appreciation** — d'Yquem 2001 was his all-time highest rating. Also tracked Rieussec, Suduiraut, Chilean late harvest, Rickety Bridge Colombar.

9. **English sparkling wine fan** — Gusbourne is clearly a favourite producer (scanned many wines). Nyetimber also rated well. Values biscuity, complex styles.

10. **Likes Chilean wine** — Perez Cruz Carmenère (4.2, "great value"), Caballo Loco Grand Cru Apalta (4.2), Calyptra Chardonnay (4.3). Appreciates value.
