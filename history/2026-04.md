## The Case

### 1. Graci Feudo di Mezzo, Etna Rosso 2022
- **Grape:** Nerello Mascalese (95%), Nerello Cappuccio (5%)
- **Region:** Etna, Sicily, Italy
- **Price:** £52.00
- **Retailer:** Berry Bros & Rudd
- **URL:** https://www.bbr.com/products-20221494325-2022-feudo-di-mezzo-etna-rosso-graci-sicily-italy
- **Why:** The splurge. Henry rates Pinot Noir among his favourite grapes (Merry Edwards 4.7, Sea Smoke 4.7) — Nerello Mascalese from Etna's volcanic slopes speaks the same language: translucent colour, lifted aromatics, silky texture, but with a mineral spine and herbal complexity that's entirely its own. Graci's 80-year-old alberello-trained vines at 600m altitude produce something genuinely haunting. Monica Larner gave this 94/100. The cherry, blood orange, sage, and granite-mineral character should thrill a palate that wants complexity over power.
- **Food pairing:** Grilled lamb chops with rosemary, wild mushroom pasta, aged pecorino
- **When to open:** Drink now with 1 hour decanting, or cellar to 2040
- **Confidence:** High — if he loves Pinot, he'll love this. The volcanic terroir adds a dimension he's never experienced.

### 2. Mas de Daumas Gassac Rouge 2021
- **Grape:** Cabernet Sauvignon (84%), Pinot Noir (5%), Merlot (2%), Petit Verdot (2%), Tannat (2%), Cabernet Franc (2%), rare varieties (3%)
- **Region:** Saint-Guilhem-le-Desert, Languedoc, France
- **Price:** £45.00
- **Retailer:** Berry Bros & Rudd
- **URL:** https://www.bbr.com/products-20218026534-2021-mas-de-daumas-gassac-rouge-st-guilhem-le-desert-cite-d-aniane-languedoc
- **Why:** The "Lafite of the Languedoc." Henry's feedback on Ridge Geyserville tells me he loves restrained, complex field blends where terroir trumps varietal character — Daumas Gassac is the French equivalent. Cabernet Sauvignon-dominated but with a wild, garrigue-scented complexity from its extraordinary terroir (glacial gravel over volcanic subsoil). James Suckling 94/100: leather, pencil lead, dried herbs, stones. At 13% ABV and only 50% barrel-aged, this is structure and finesse, not power. Southern France is a push region he hasn't explored.
- **Food pairing:** Slow-roasted shoulder of lamb, cassoulet, aged Comté
- **When to open:** Cellar ideally — drinking window 2026-2038. Can open now with long decanting but will reward patience.
- **Confidence:** High — this is exactly the kind of wine his palate craves: complex, structured, not fruit-forward, incredible terroir story.

### 3. Berry Bros. & Rudd Swartland Red by The Sadie Family Wines 2023
- **Grape:** Syrah/Shiraz (primary)
- **Region:** Swartland, South Africa
- **Price:** £35.00
- **Retailer:** Berry Bros & Rudd
- **URL:** https://www.bbr.com/products-20238170761-2023-berry-bros-and-rudd-swartland-red-by-the-sadie-family-wines-south-africa

This BBR exclusive is made from his newly planted vineyards: red and black berries with herbal freshness, cranberry tartness, powdery tannins. At 13% ABV, it's elegant rather than burly. This is his comfort zone elevated to a level he may not have tried. Assessed by Catriona Felstead MW.
- **Food pairing:** Braised oxtail, bobotie, grilled ribeye with chimichurri
- **When to open:** Drink now — ready and drinking beautifully 2024-2026
- **Confidence:** Very high — he knows and loves this region, this is its finest producer

### 4. Thymiopoulos Earth and Sky, Naoussa Xinomavro 2023
- **Grape:** Xinomavro
- **Region:** Naoussa, Macedonia, Greece
- **Price:** £25.00
- **Retailer:** The Wine Society
- **URL:** https://www.thewinesociety.com/product/earth-and-sky-naoussa-thymiopoulos-2023-en.aspx
- **Why:** The star discovery. Xinomavro — "sour black" — is Greece's answer to Nebbiolo: tannic, high-acid, complex, age-worthy. Thymiopoulos is the leading young biodynamic producer in Naoussa, farming 50-year-old vines on chalky, green slate, and red marble soils. Cherry, black tea, tomato leaf, blackberry — with dusty tannins and bright acidity that Jancis Robinson called "exciting" and "good value." Henry's taste profile flags Greece as unexplored territory, and his love of structured, complex reds makes this a natural fit. Often compared to top Barolo/Barbaresco. Drinking window to 2035.
- **Food pairing:** Moussaka, slow-roasted lamb, grilled aubergine
- **When to open:** Drink now with 1-2 hours decanting, or cellar to 2035
- **Confidence:** Medium-high — Xinomavro's tannic structure can be challenging, but he embraced Nebbiolo last month, and this has a similar DNA

### 5. Brundlmayer Terrassen Riesling, Kamptal 2023
- **Grape:** Riesling
- **Region:** Kamptal, Austria
- **Price:** £19.00
- **Retailer:** The Wine Society
- **URL:** https://www.thewinesociety.com/product/brundlmayer-terrassen-riesling-kamptal-2023-en.aspx
- **Why:** The white discovery. Austrian Riesling is on the push list, and Willi Brundlmayer is arguably Austria's greatest winemaker — a pioneer who cemented the country's reputation for world-class dry Riesling. This has real weight at 13% ABV, with stone fruit, white pepper, and stony minerality that lingers. It's organic, dry, and complex — not a lightweight aperitif wine. Henry barely explores whites outside Chardonnay, but this has enough body and texture to bridge the gap while opening up a completely new flavour world. Low stock — glad we caught it.
- **Food pairing:** Thai green curry, grilled prawns, charcuterie, dim sum
- **When to open:** Drink now, or cellar to 2033
- **Confidence:** Medium — genuine stretch, but the weight and complexity should appeal to his palate

### 6. The Society's Exhibition Douro 2022 (Quinta do Vale Meao)
- **Grape:** Touriga Franca (predominantly), Baga (~15%)
- **Region:** Douro, Portugal
- **Price:** £16.50
- **Retailer:** The Wine Society
- **URL:** https://www.thewinesociety.com/product/the-societys-exhibition-douro-2022-en.aspx
- **Why:** The value bomb. Quinta do Vale Meao is Portuguese wine royalty — the estate was developed in 1877 by Antonia Adelaide Ferreira, and it's the original home of Barca-Velha (Portugal's most celebrated red, which sells for £700+ at BBR). This Exhibition bottling is made by her great-great-grandson Francisco Javier de Olazabal, foot-trodden in century-old granite lagares. Dark fruit, rose petal, lavender, broad silky palate. At £16.50, this is one of the most absurd quality-to-price ratios in wine. Portugal is a push region and Henry values wines that "drink like 3x the price" — this delivers exactly that.
- **Food pairing:** Reblochon, roast duck, hearty bean stews
- **When to open:** Drink now, or hold to 2031
- **Confidence:** High — the style (structured, complex, great value) is squarely his profile, just from a new region
